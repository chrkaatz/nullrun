# Conformance run — build 0.8.0

Date: 2026-09-09 · Spec: 0.8.0 · `nullrun.html`
Reported by playtest: "the hard ones don't seem doable" (layer 3, three offers, all paying 111¤).

## What the screenshot actually showed

Two separate bugs, both introduced by the 0.5.0 alarm cut.

**1. Payout carried no per-node term.** The formula was
`payoutFlat + payoutScaled × layerScale + alarmCost × payoutAlarm`. Cutting alarm removed the
last term — the only one that differed between offers in a layer. Measured: **identical
payouts in 100% of 700 sampled layers, maximum spread ever observed: 0.** The three-way
choice was decorative; there was never a reason to take the harder node.

**2. `I2` was deleted for mentioning alarm.** It read "every offer layer presents at least one
node that does not raise alarm". Its *function* was: guarantee one offer the player can take.
Offers draw three constructs from four, so COLD STORAGE — the only one clearable with a
starting roster — was absent from a quarter of layers. Those layers were unwinnable on arrival.

**3. CORPSEC SWEEP was unclearable at every layer by any roster**, including the best possible
six contacts. It was not difficult content; it was impossible content.

**4. K15 never noticed**, because its policy always took the softest node. Across 200 runs it
entered COLD STORAGE 579 times and ICE WALL 201 times — HUNTER and CORPSEC SWEEP **zero times**.
The 31% win rate certified in 0.7.0 was measured on a game with half its content unreachable.

## The pattern, for the third time

| version | cut | function lost silently |
|---|---|---|
| 0.3.0 | FR-6 free step | renewable damage |
| 0.3.0 | FR-11 repair | renewable firewall |
| 0.5.0 | I2, alarm | a takeable node; payout differentiation |

Each time a requirement was phrased as a *mechanism*, the mechanism was cut, and the
*function* went with it unnoticed. M2 now states the rule directly.

## Fixes

- Payout scales with the node's own integrity (FR-10). Spread now 47–97 within a layer.
- `I2` restored, stated as a function and enforced during generation, drawing no extra
  randomness so NFR-2 holds.
- CORPSEC SWEEP integrity 111 → 95.
- `K2` now also requires differing payouts and one takeable node.
- `K15` now requires the policy to enter every construct.
- Roster cap 6 → 8 (§T). At 6 the roster grew from 5 to a mean of 5.9 across an entire run —
  burst 29 → 35 — so hard nodes opened only 11% of the time. At 8 they open 30%, and all four
  constructs get fought.

## Results

K1–K8, K13, K14, K16 pass. K2 passes across 350 layers: distinct constructs, differing
payouts, one takeable. Layer 3 now reads
`COLD 65 / 138¤ · ICE WALL 100 / 180¤ · CORPSEC 122 / 206¤`.

K15: win 63%, encounters 3.8 turns, 0% instant, all four constructs entered — in band.

## K17 FAILS, and the reason is structural

Trace ends 0% of runs at these values, so **this rebuild is not accepted under §K.**

The cause is a coupling, not a number: `damageBudget = burst + breach × turnsBeforeTrace`.
Raising the trace rate shrinks the turn budget, which shrinks which nodes are clearable.
Measured across the joint space:

| cap · trace/turn | win | enc turns | hard takeable | trace deaths | constructs met |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 · 15 | 63% | 3.8 | 30% | **0%** | 4 |
| 8 · 18 | 69% | 2.6 | 4% | 32% | 2 |
| 8 · 21 | 7% | 2.6 | 4% | 94% | 2 |
| 8 · 24 | 0% | 2.2 | 5% | 100% | 2 |

**A biting clock and reachable hard nodes are mutually exclusive at these values.** No cell
satisfies both. This needs a rule change, not a tune — trace and clearability must stop
reading from the same quantity.

## Also unresolved: the reward for risk is still flat

Payouts now differ, but credits do nothing inside a run — FR-12 gives one contact or repair
regardless of which node was cleared. The risk/reward axis is honest on the offers screen and
still absent in play.
