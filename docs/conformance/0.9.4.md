# Conformance run — build 0.9.4

Date: 2026-09-10 · Spec: 0.9.4 · breach growth 2 → 1.5

Taken up as `O11`. Found something worse on the way.

## K16 has been passing on a rigged harness since 0.4.0

The harness ran `S.fw = 9999; S.trace = 0;` every turn — falsifying **both** constraints that
make the check mean anything. Run honestly, with nothing faked:

```
layer-1 node, 51 integrity, every contact burned
breach alone: 28 damage across the 7 turns trace allows
result: run ends TRACED on turn 8, construct still at 19
```

K16 as worded — *"a layer-1 node is clearable by breach alone"* — was **false for six
versions** and reported PASS every time.

## And the check contradicted the requirement it guarded

`FR-5` states breach "stays too weak to carry an encounter, only to extend one."
`K16` demanded breach **can** carry one. They cannot both hold. The rigged harness concealed
the contradiction rather than surfacing it, which is precisely what a conformance check exists
to prevent.

`K16` was the wrong check. `I8` guarantees a damage source that does not burn, so the run is
not decided on entry — not that the source suffices alone. Rewritten to test that: with every
contact burned, breach still reduces the construct each turn, and reach exceeds roster burst by
a margin growing with the turns trace allows.

## FR-5 was also genuinely violated — that was `O11`

| layer | breach alone | softest node | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 28 | 51 | ok |
| 4 | 70 | 72 | ok |
| **5** | **84** | **80** | **carried** |
| 6 | 98 | 87 | carried |
| 7 | 112 | 94 | carried |

From layer 5 the free action alone cleared the softest node, and breach did **71% of all
damage by layer 8**, out-damaging 8 of 9 damage contacts. The roster was becoming decoration
and P3.3 with it.

Growth cut 2 → 1.5. Breach now falls short at every layer: 28/51 … 91/94.

`K19` added so the requirement checks itself rather than relying on someone noticing.

## Results — all run without falsifying state

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| **K19 breach cannot carry** | **PASS — short at every layer** |
| **K16 renewable, honestly** | **PASS — reduces the construct each turn; reach 57 vs burst 29** |
| K15 viability | PASS — win 45%, 3.3 turns, 0% instant, all 4 constructs |
| K17 trace binds | PASS — 100% of careless runs traced |

Cost: hard nodes are takeable 8% of the time, down from 31%. FR-5 compliance was worth more
than the variety, but the thinness is real and unresolved.

## Method changes

§K now states: *a harness may not modify state the check depends on.* That rule is what this
version cost to learn.

Per-check rationale moved out of §K into these records, with one guard line kept in the spec
warning that several checks are worded against a specific past failure and must not be
simplified. Same split as the change log in 0.9.0: the requirement stays, the argument moves.

## Not addressed

`O12` — breach costs no trace. Probed: making it cost trace shortens the turn budget and puts
`K16` and `I8` under pressure from the other side. Left open.

Re-cutting contacts around what breach cannot do — strip, firewall, trace — is the live half
of `O11` and untouched. Of 14 contacts, 4 are pure damage.
