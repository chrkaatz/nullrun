## Effect

| Mean layers cleared | 1.0 | **2.6** |
| Deaths by firewall | 59% | 96% |

The replenishment ratio is resolved: the roster no longer needs replenishing, so a payout
contact became permanent growth rather than a patch.

K17 regressed to 4% — runs no longer live long enough in one node for trace to strike, since
firewall now ends them first. The trace rate tuned in 0.5.0 was fitted to the old baseline,
exactly as that run warned it would need refitting.

## The constraint moved again, and the shape repeated

Repair was swept 12 → 24 and healing policy from panic to top-up: **mean layers stayed at
2.4–2.5 in every cell.** Firewall was the symptom, not the constraint.

The arithmetic:

```
turns per node before trace     7
roster burst (armour 0)        29
breach over those turns    4 × 7 = 28
TOTAL DAMAGE PER NODE          57
```

| layer | softest node | clearable |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 34 | yes |
| 5 | 53 | yes |
| 6 | 58 | **no** |
| 7 | 63 | **no** |

The toughest node is unclearable at every layer. The run is mathematically capped at layer 5.

This is the original bug's shape one level up: **a capped resource against a growing
requirement.** First roster-bounded damage per encounter; now node-bounded damage against
layer-scaled integrity. `breach × turns` is the only term that can grow.

## A coordinated retune exists

Single levers cannot satisfy K15 and K17 together: raising integrity lengthens encounters but
nothing can be finished; raising breach wins by ending encounters instantly. Firewall,
intents, integrity and breach are mutually over-constrained and must move together.

Searched jointly, three configurations satisfy every criterion (win 15–65%, encounters ≥2.5
turns, instant kills ≤10%, trace still killing):

| config | win | layers | enc turns | instant | trace deaths |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fw 48, intents ×0.7, integ ×1.5, breach 4+2, repair 20 | 17% | 3.2 | 3.9 | 0% | 50% |
| **fw 60, intents ×0.6, integ ×1.5, breach 4+2, repair 24** | **34%** | 3.8 | 3.6 | 0% | 63% |
| fw 72, intents ×0.5, integ ×2.0, breach 6+3, repair 28 | 41% | 3.9 | 4.3 | 0% | 59% |

Every element is §T or §C — an iterate — **except breach scaling, which is a rule change.**
