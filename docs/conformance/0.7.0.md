## The change

Breach grows: `breach damage + breach growth × (layer − 1)` → 4, 6, 8 … 18 at layer 8.
Firewall 24 → 60, repair 12 → 24, construct integrity ×1.5, intent values ×0.6.

Breach had to grow because integrity does. Damage per node was capped at 57 while layer 6's
softest node reached 58, so the run was mathematically capped at layer 5.

## Why the four numbers moved together

Single levers could not satisfy K15 and K17 at once:

- raising integrity lengthened encounters but nothing could be finished (0% wins at 3.8 turns)
- raising breach won by ending encounters instantly (40% wins at 1.2 turns, trace back to 0%)

Firewall, intent damage, integrity and breach are mutually over-constrained: encounter length
is bounded by `firewall ÷ intent damage`, and required damage by `integrity − burst`. Solving
them jointly found three valid configurations; the middle one shipped.

## K15 was tightened first

Scaling breach alone passed the old K15 while collapsing encounters to about one turn — the
same hollow win 0.3.1 produced. K15 now requires a 15–65% win rate, encounters averaging at
least 2.5 turns, and under a tenth ending before a turn is taken.

Had K15 not been tightened, this build would have shipped at 40% wins and one-turn fights,
and the checklist would have certified a worse game than the one before it.

## Defect found and fixed

`NEXT: HOLD` and `NEXT: RESTORE` rendered with no value, violating NFR-3 ("every intent states
its value in text"). Now `NEXT: HOLD 0` and `NEXT: RESTORE +2 ARMOUR`. Build-only patch, no
spec change — a `defect` under M3.

## Standing risk

`O12`: breach grows while contact damage does not, so by layer 6 the free action out-damages
most of the roster. Contacts will have to earn their slot on effects breach has no access to.
Unmeasured, and the most likely next structural failure.
