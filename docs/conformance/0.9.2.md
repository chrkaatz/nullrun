# Conformance run — build 0.9.2

Date: 2026-09-10 · Spec: 0.9.2 · `nullrun.html` **unchanged from 0.9.1**

`K17` failed from 0.8.0 through 0.9.1. The fix is to the check, not the build — and the
evidence for that claim is below, because "rewrite the failing check" is exactly how a
checklist gets gamed.

## K17 as written could only pass by punishing correct play

The old check read: *"Trace ends some runs across 200 seeds."* It was measured against a
careful policy — one that reads reach and takes nodes it can clear. Such a policy finishes
encounters in 3.8 of the 7 turns trace allows, so trace never fires. Making it fire would mean
tuning the game until the clock kills players who chose correctly.

Attempts to tune into a pass, holding reach constant by raising breach as the turn budget
shrank:

| trace/turn · breach | turns allowed | win | hard takeable | traced |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 · 4+2 | 7 | 57% | 33% | 0% |
| 18 · 6+3 | 5 | 100% | 50% | 0% |
| 21 · 8+3 | 5 | 100% | 65% | 0% |
| 24 · 10+4 | 4 | 100% | 69% | 0% |

Compensating breach keeps every node reachable, so fights end early and the clock still never
strikes. No cell passes. The coupling was never the whole story.

## What trace actually does

Measured three ways:

| probe | result |
|---|---|
| careful play, trace on | win 64%, 6.9 layers, **traced 0%** |
| **greedy play ignoring reach** | **traced 100%** |
| careful play, trace removed | win 64% → **0%**, layers 6.9 → **0** |

Trace is entirely load-bearing. It bounds every encounter, which is what makes reach finite
and the FR-10 signal meaningful; remove it and reach becomes unbounded, every node reads as
clearable, and outcomes collapse. It also ends **100%** of runs that ignore reach.

A clock that never catches careful play is a clock working correctly.

## Why this is not gaming the checklist

The failure K17 exists to prevent is alarm's: content that does nothing. The two are
distinguishable by measurement, and the new check separates them.

| | fired against careful play | fired against careless play | removing it changed outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| alarm (0.3.1–0.4.1) | 0% | 0% | **no** |
| trace (0.9.x) | 0% | **100%** | **yes, totally** |

The old check could not tell these apart — both show 0% against a careful policy. **The new
check fails alarm and passes trace**, which is the discrimination it was always meant to make.
It is strictly stronger on the property that matters, and weaker only on a proxy that
mistook correct design for a defect.

## K17 rewritten

> **`K17` Trace binds.** A policy that ignores reach and chases payout is ended by trace across
> 200 seeds, and removing trace changes outcomes materially. A clock that binds nothing is the
> alarm system again; one that never catches careful play is working as intended.
